Examples from the 15+ critical gaps

  • No firm definition of "match" and "unmatch" status; staff interpreted them differently.
  • Reconciling item categories were too general, so fraud patterns went undetected.
  • No resolution deadline per category; items stayed open indefinitely.
  • Bank reconciliation was only monthly; fraud gaps could stay hidden for weeks.
2 · Identify Risks

10 fraud scenarios mapped to specific controls

Not just "fixing the SOPs", but closing every gap with auditable controls. Some of them:

Account switching

Vendor bank account changed. Control: bank confirmation verification and approval chain.

Duplicate payment

Paying twice. Control: duplicate check and authorization matrix.

Inventory manipulation

Hidden shortages. Control: goods quarantine and variance monitoring.

Manual journal override

Unreviewed journals. Control: journal voucher form and approval chain.

Unauthorized discount

Discounts without approval. Control: approval log and approval hierarchy.

FAQ

About financial evaluation

What is Business Financial Evaluation?

Business Financial Evaluation (BFE) is an evaluation that helps a company understand its financial position and identify priority areas. The results are discussed in language management can readily use to decide the next steps.

What is the difference between Business Financial Evaluation and a Fractional CFO?

Business Financial Evaluation helps you understand your financial position and identify priority areas. A Fractional CFO brings that financial perspective into ongoing support for management decisions.

What is the Corporate Financial Diagnostic?

The Corporate Financial Diagnostic is the five-stage framework we use in our evaluations: Diagnose (mapping the financial position and processes), Identify Risks (finding control gaps and risk points), Design Controls (designing controls and supporting tools), Implementation (supporting a phased rollout), and Monitoring (tracking and refining).

We already have finance SOPs and a tax consultant. Is an evaluation still relevant?

Often, yes. In our F&B manufacturer case study, the client already had SOPs and a tax consultant, yet the evaluation still found more than 15 critical gaps, such as unclear definitions of reconciliation status and bank reconciliations performed only monthly.

How long does the evaluation take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the company. As an illustration, the evaluation and control design for one F&B manufacturer was followed by about 2 months of implementation support.

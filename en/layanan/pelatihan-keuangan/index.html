FAQ

About our training

What training topics are available?

Topics we have delivered include retirement planning, financial planning, business financial management and break-even (BEP) calculation, financial literacy and protection from digital scams, planning and feasibility studies for founders, and SBN treasury strategy for the banking sector. Content can be tailored to the participant profile.

Who has used Harsha Advisory's training?

Clients include British Petroleum, Petronas, Kangean Energy, Bank Indonesia, Komdigi, GoPay, BDI Denpasar, and BPRs (rural banks).

Why should companies pay attention to employee financial wellness?

Good financial wellness correlates with company productivity. When employees better understand their financial situation and options, companies have a strong reason to care about their financial well-being.

How do participants rate the training?

86% of participants rated our training content as highly relevant to their business needs.

Can the training be held at our company's premises?

Yes. Most of our training is delivered as in-house workshops at the client's premises or a venue of their choice. Format and duration are agreed after a needs discussion.

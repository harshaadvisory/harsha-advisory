Illustrative use cases

How the approach works in practice

The following scenarios are illustrative and describe our approach; actual work is always tailored to the client's context.

Use case 1

Regional BPR with IDR 50 billion in idle funds

A BPR (rural bank) with significant reserves wants to optimize yield without raising its risk profile, and is exploring green SBN (Indonesian government securities) for diversification.

  • Assessment of ALM profile and risk appetite
  • Yield curve analysis of conventional and green SBN
  • Stress testing of benchmark interest rate scenarios
  • Allocation framework recommendations and a POJK compliance check for BPRs

Output: treasury policy document, implementation roadmap, monitoring framework.

Use case 2

Mid-sized F&B business, 3 outlets, applying for green financing

A restaurant with sustainability commitments (local sourcing, energy efficiency) wants to access green financing or sustainable corporate financing from a bank.

  • Assessment of current sustainability practices
  • Mapping to criteria recognized by banks
  • Documentation and financial preparation
  • Introductions to relevant partner banks and a post-financing monitoring framework

Output: ESG readiness report, financing application package, monitoring dashboard.

Use case 3

Family business, ESG board briefing

A second-generation board wants to understand the ESG implications for its five-year strategy, without jumping straight into full reporting.

  • Half-day workshop with the board
  • Concise materiality assessment
  • Financial impact analysis of several strategic options
  • Phased three-year roadmap

Output: board briefing deck, materiality matrix, strategic recommendation document.

FAQ

About sustainable finance

Does Harsha Advisory provide full sustainability reporting?

Not on our own. For full sustainability reporting (comprehensive POJK 51 compliance), TCFD, carbon markets, and Scope 3 emissions accounting, we work with certified ESG consultants and act as the financial adviser who integrates their results into your financial strategy.

What are green SBN and why are they relevant for BPRs?

Green SBN (government green bonds or green sukuk) are Indonesian government securities whose proceeds are allocated to environmentally friendly projects. For BPRs (rural banks), green SBN can serve as a treasury diversification instrument. We analyze the yield curves of conventional and green SBN, run stress tests, and check compliance with the POJK (OJK regulations) that apply to BPRs.

Automated COGS Calculation Template for Businesses

Calculate your cost of production the way cost accountants do, without needing to understand accounting.

What this template tells you

  • The true cost of making one unit of each product
  • How much of your COGS (HPP) comes from materials, labor and overhead
  • The gap between the cost according to your recipe and the cost actually incurred, so material waste shows up quickly
  • The minimum selling price needed to hit your target margin
  • Which products are the most profitable and which need a second look
  • Your operating profit and the minimum sales needed to avoid a loss
  • An easy-to-read one-page summary for making decisions

Easy to use and customizable

  • Step-by-step guide to filling it in
  • All sheets are linked: enter your data once and the results are calculated automatically
  • You can add your own products, raw materials and cost items to fit your business
  • Automatic checks that flag data that is incomplete or looks unusual

What you receive

  • A blank file, ready to fill in with your company's data
  • A sample file, to see how the template works before you start

Ideal for: manufacturing and production companies that make or assemble their own products and want to calculate COGS correctly.

Before you buy

The template is a Microsoft Excel file. Just fill in the yellow cells and everything else is calculated automatically.

This template is a calculation tool, not a substitute for advice from an accountant or tax consultant.

Automated Financial Statement Template for Service Businesses (Excel)

Keep tidier transaction records, know your service margins, and understand your company's financial position, all in one Excel file.

Payment is processed securely by Lynk.id.

Just enter your opening balances and record your transactions. The financial statements update automatically based on what you enter.

What's included?

  • Transaction Records, for recording service revenue, salary and commission payments, operating expenses and other financial transactions.
  • Automated Financial Statements, covering the General Ledger, Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement.
  • Service Business Income Statement, which separates the direct costs of delivering your services (service staff, consumables, third-party services) from office expenses, so your service margin is clearly visible.
  • Customer Advance Records, for down payments, packages, memberships or course fees paid before the service is delivered.
  • Supplies Stock Cards (optional), for businesses that keep supplies in stock, such as salons, clinics, repair shops or laundries.
  • Financial Performance Summary, showing revenue, profit, cash, assets, liabilities and equity on one dashboard.
  • Financial Ratio Analysis, to help you read your company's liquidity, profitability and overall financial health.
  • Step-by-Step Guide, so users can easily understand how to fill in and use each worksheet.

Why this template

  • Two files: a blank file ready to fill in, and a sample file that shows how the template works.
  • Account names can be adjusted to the terms your business uses, and you can add new accounts yourself, which flow into the reports automatically.
  • Report formulas are already built in.
  • Includes checks on transactions and on whether the statements balance.
  • One file per reporting period: monthly, quarterly or annual.

Who is it for?

Owners of service companies, management, and admin and finance teams who want more structured record-keeping and financial statements that are easy to understand.

Suitable for many kinds of service businesses, such as salons and beauty clinics, repair shops, laundries, training centers, agencies and consulting firms.

Manage your company's numbers more clearly, so financial decisions are not based on guesswork alone.

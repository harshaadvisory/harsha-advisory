Personal Financial Planning Template

Know where your family's finances stand, without doing the math by hand.

This Excel template brings together your budget plan, transaction records, net worth statement, financial goals and a financial health check in one file.

Key feature: financial health check-up

Get a financial health score based on ratios commonly used by financial planners. Each indicator is given a status straight away, along with suggested next steps. You can also adjust the ratio thresholds yourself.

What you'll find out

  • Where your money goes each month, and which categories go over budget
  • What your net worth is today, and how your assets and debts are made up
  • Whether your emergency fund is large enough
  • How much you need to set aside each month for your children's education, retirement or other goals, with inflation already taken into account
  • Whether your contributions to each goal are on track or falling short
  • Your financial position month by month and as a yearly average, on one summary page

Easy to use and customizable

  • Step-by-step guide to the workflow
  • Pick transaction categories from a drop-down list
  • Add your own budget, asset, debt and financial goal categories
  • Record assets and debts separately for you and your partner
  • Fill in the yellow cells and the rest is calculated automatically. No formula skills needed.

Ideal for

  • Couples and families who want to start keeping their finances in order
  • People who already track their spending but don't yet know whether their finances are healthy
  • People with goals such as their children's education, retirement or a holiday, who don't yet know how much to set aside

What you get

  • A blank file, ready to fill in with your own financial data
  • A sample file with a family's data, so you can see right away how it works

Microsoft Excel format, prepared by Harsha Advisory.

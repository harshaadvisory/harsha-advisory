FAQ

Questions we often receive

Haven't found your answer? Ask us directly at info@harshaadvisory.com.

What is a Fractional CFO?

A Fractional CFO provides financial support that helps management read the company's financial position and assess important decisions with greater focus. This approach is useful when a company needs a deeper financial perspective in its decision-making.

When does a company need a Fractional CFO?

When financial statements are available but decisions on cash, profitability and growth still need clearer analysis. The service is also relevant when management wants to build more structured financial discussions.

What is the difference between a Fractional CFO and Business Financial Evaluation?

Business Financial Evaluation helps you understand your financial position and identify priority areas. A Fractional CFO brings that financial perspective into ongoing support for management decisions.

Can a company that already has a finance team still use Harsha's services?

Absolutely. A Fractional CFO strengthens your internal finance team through deeper analysis, financial performance reviews, and a strategic perspective. That way, management can make sharper business decisions without adding a full-time CFO position.

Does Harsha offer training programs for companies?

Yes. Financial Training can be tailored to the needs of management and company participants, including topics on financial understanding and financial planning.

How much do Harsha Advisory's services cost?

Fees depend on the service and the scope of work. Fractional CFO support starts from IDR 5 million per month with a minimum engagement period of 3 months, while Business Financial Evaluation and Financial Training are priced according to their scope. The 30-minute initial consultation is free, so we can discuss your company's needs first.

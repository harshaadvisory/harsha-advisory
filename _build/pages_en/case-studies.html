Corporate Financial Diagnostic & Advisory

Client: F&B manufacturer

The client already had finance SOPs and a tax consultant, but control gaps still left it exposed to leakage. We diagnosed the Finance, Accounting, and Tax cycles, mapped 10 fraud scenarios to specific controls, and delivered 20 control templates along with an implementation roadmap.

Reconstruction of Books & Financial Statements

Client: F&B business

Four financial years (2022 to 2025) with no structured bookkeeping, and bank statements as the only available data. We rebuilt the entire record from scratch: asset register, depreciation, adjusting journal entries, profit and loss, and balance sheet, in both annual and monthly versions.

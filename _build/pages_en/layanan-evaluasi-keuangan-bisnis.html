1 · Diagnose

Findings: where the risk lies

Initial assessment scores, before any improvements:

AreaInitial score
Procedure completeness60%
Internal control & fraud prevention40%
Documentation & audit trail50%

Scores reflect the client's position at diagnosis, not results after improvement.

Examples from the 15+ critical gaps

  • No firm definition of "match" and "unmatch" status; staff interpreted them differently.
  • Reconciling item categories were too general, so fraud patterns went undetected.
  • No resolution deadline per category; items stayed open indefinitely.
  • Bank reconciliation was only monthly; fraud gaps could stay hidden for weeks.
2 · Identify Risks

10 fraud scenarios mapped to specific controls

Not just "fixing the SOPs", but closing every gap with auditable controls. Some of them:

Account switching

Vendor bank account changed. Control: bank confirmation verification and approval chain.

Duplicate payment

Paying twice. Control: duplicate check and authorization matrix.

Inventory manipulation

Hidden shortages. Control: goods quarantine and variance monitoring.

Manual journal override

Unreviewed journals. Control: journal voucher form and approval chain.

Unauthorized discount

Discounts without approval. Control: approval log and approval hierarchy.

+ 5 more scenarios

Including period juggling and purchase orders without approval.

3 · Design Controls

What we delivered

  • 20 control templates in 2 packages, covering cash and receivables, sales, inventory and closing controls.
  • Stronger monthly closing: fixing 4 key weaknesses and adding 11 mandatory controls.
  • Stronger bank reconciliation: tolerance threshold, escalation matrix, segregation of duties and daily red flags.
Business benefits

Faster closing

Monthly closing that is more structured and on time.

Lower fraud risk

Control gaps closed and issues detected earlier.

Clear accountability

Preparer separate from reviewer, with a firm approval trail.

Better audit readiness

Documentation aligned with PSAK (Indonesian accounting standards)/IFRS.
